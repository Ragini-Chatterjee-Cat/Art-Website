1. Introduction & Overview

This manual provides complete instructions for implementing an AI-powered chatbot on your art portfolio website "Inside the Paintbox". The chatbot uses RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) technology to answer visitor questions about your artwork intelligently and accurately.

1.1 What is RAG?

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a technique that combines information retrieval with AI text generation. Instead of relying solely on an AI model's training data, RAG first searches your own documents (artwork descriptions) to find relevant information, then uses that context to generate accurate, personalized responses.

Why RAG for Your Art Website?
A standard AI chatbot doesn't know anything about YOUR specific paintings. RAG allows the AI to access your artwork descriptions, inspiration notes, and other content to provide accurate, personalized responses about your art.

Traditional Chatbot vs RAG Chatbot

Traditional Chatbot RAG Chatbot (Your System)
Uses only general AI knowledge Uses YOUR artwork descriptions + AI
Generic responses about art Specific responses about YOUR paintings
Can't explain your inspiration Explains exactly what inspired each piece
Doesn't know your series names Knows all your series: Mythical, In Purple, etc.

2.3 Component Explanations

Vector Embeddings (What are they?)

Embeddings are numerical representations of text that capture semantic meaning. Similar texts have similar embeddings. This allows the system to find artwork descriptions that are semantically related to a question, even if they don't share exact words.

Example:
"What inspired Voices?" and "Tell me about the Voices painting" have different words but similar embeddings because they mean similar things. The system finds relevant content based on meaning, not just keyword matching.

Vector Database (ChromaDB)

ChromaDB stores your artwork descriptions as vectors and enables fast similarity searches. When indexed, each artwork page becomes a searchable entry that can be retrieved when relevant questions are asked.

Large Language Model (Llama 3 via Groq)

The LLM is the "brain" that generates human-like responses. Groq provides free access to Meta's Llama 3.3 70B model, which is powerful enough to understand context and generate articulate responses about your artwork.

6. Troubleshooting

Problem Possible Cause Solution
"Couldn't connect to my brain" Backend not running or wrong URL 1. Check Render dashboard - is service running?
2. Verify API_URL in chat.js matches your Render URL
3. Check browser console for errors
"GROQ_API_KEY not set" Missing environment variable Add GROQ_API_KEY in Render dashboard → Environment
No artworks indexed (count: 0) Document loader can't find HTML files 1. Check artwork HTML files exist
2. Call /reindex endpoint
3. Check Render logs for errors
Slow first response (~30 seconds) Render free tier sleeping Normal behavior - server wakes up on first request
CORS error in browser console Cross-origin request blocked Verify CORS settings in app.py include your Netlify domain
Chat button doesn't open chat JavaScript error Check browser console for errors in chat.js
Generic/wrong answers Artworks not properly indexed 1. Check if HTML files have content in <p> tags
2. Reindex: POST to /reindex endpoint
